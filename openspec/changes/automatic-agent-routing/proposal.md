## Why

Light research and isolated implementations should consume fewer resources while the owner can inspect model choices and usage.

## What

Use local Kev to choose Luna/medium, Sol/medium or Sol/high for Research and Apply. Keep Planner and Author on their configured Sol/high profile. Pin the decision to each run across retries and resumes. High-impact tasks and unavailable/invalid routing fall back to Sol/high; ambiguous Luna choices fall back to Sol/medium.

Persist a separate local audit journal for all roles with numeric routing evidence, task fingerprints, attempts, latency, tool calls and CLI-reported token usage. Show cumulative totals and recent records in Desk. Actual billing stays unknown; the standard API text-token estimate is explicitly separate from subscription usage. Owner approvals and domain decisions keep their existing journals.
